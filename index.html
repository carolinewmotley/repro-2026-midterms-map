Why these results matter

Governors sign or veto abortion legislation, and in many states they appoint the judges who decide challenges to abortion laws. U.S. senators vote on federal abortion legislation and confirm federal judges. Ballot measures can write abortion protections into a state constitution, or take them out. A change in who holds office does not change a state's law by itself, so the news feed for each state tracks what new officials and legislatures do next.
